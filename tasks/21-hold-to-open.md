# 21 — Hold on a song before it opens

The wait is now the slide plus the skin's 133ms, which is 299ms at 1x.
That stops a box opening while the wheel is still moving, but it still
opens the moment you pause, and what is wanted is a deliberate hold.

## What it changes

The delay after the wheel comes to rest, which is the part that is
"how long you have to hold". Longer than the skin's, on purpose.

## How it will be shown to work

Scroll at a normal pace and nothing opens; stop, and it takes a beat.
